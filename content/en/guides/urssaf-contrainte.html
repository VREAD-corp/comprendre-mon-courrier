URSSAF (social contributions)

You received an URSSAF enforcement order (contrainte)

In short

A contrainte is issued when a formal notice was not acted on. It is usually served by a judicial officer (commissaire de justice). Without opposition, it has the effect of a court judgment: URSSAF can then seize bank accounts or property.

Deadlines to check

  • You have 15 days from notification or service to file an opposition.
  • The opposition is filed with the registry of the competent court (social division of the judicial court where you live) or by registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt; it must give reasons and include a copy of the contrainte. The contrainte itself must state this deadline and the court.

What to do

  • Note the date of notification or service.
  • If you disagree, file an opposition within 15 days, explaining your reasons and attaching a copy of the contrainte.
  • If you accept the debt, contact URSSAF for a payment plan.
  • Get help: a point-justice or a lawyer can guide you.

Watch out: 15 days is very short: act as soon as you receive it.

Sources and official links
