Fines and driving licence

You received an increased fixed fine (amende forfaitaire majorée)

In short

A fine is « majorée » (increased) when the original fine was neither paid nor challenged in time. The amount is higher, and the public treasury can then use enforced collection.

Deadlines to check

  • Claim (réclamation): within 30 days of the notice being sent (3 months if it was sent by registered letter). You can no longer file a claim once you have started paying.
  • Quick payment: paying within 30 days (45 days online) gives a 20% reduction.

What to do

  • Ask yourself why you did not receive the original notice (address change not updated on your vehicle registration?).
  • If you dispute it, file the claim within the deadline, following the instructions on the notice (online on the ANTAI website or by post).
  • If you pay, use only the methods on the notice or amendes.gouv.fr.

Watch out: Update the address on your vehicle registration certificate: that is where notices are sent.
