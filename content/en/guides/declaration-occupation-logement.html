The tax office asks you to declare how your home is occupied

In short

Property owners must tell the tax office how their homes are used (main home, second home, rented, empty). This is used to calculate the housing tax (taxe d'habitation), which now mainly applies to second homes.

Deadlines to check

  • The occupancy declaration is made in « Gérer mes biens immobiliers » on impots.gouv.fr. It was due before 1 July 2026 for owners who had never filed it or whose situation had changed; otherwise only when something changes.
  • 2026 housing tax on second homes: pay before 15 December 2026.

What to do

  • Log in to your personal account, section « Biens immobiliers ».
  • Check each property and how it was occupied on 1 January; correct if needed.
  • If you receive a housing tax bill for your main home, file a claim through secure messaging.

Watch out: A missing or wrong declaration can lead to a fine: correct it as soon as possible.
