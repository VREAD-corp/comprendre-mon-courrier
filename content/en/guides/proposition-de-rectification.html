Taxes

You received a proposed tax adjustment (proposition de rectification)

In short

After an audit, the tax office proposes to correct your tax and explains why. It is a proposal: you can accept it or send your comments.

Deadlines to check

  • You have 30 days from receipt to reply.
  • This can be extended by 30 days if you ask and your request arrives before the first deadline ends.
  • If you do not reply in time, you are treated as having accepted the proposal.

What to do

  • Note the date you received it (the registered-mail receipt is the reference).
  • If you need time, ask for the extension in writing, straight away.
  • You have the right to be assisted by an adviser of your choice (lawyer, accountant), as the audited taxpayer's charter states.
  • Reply in writing, point by point, with your supporting documents.

Watch out: This letter can have major financial consequences. Get help from a professional; a point-justice can guide you for free.
