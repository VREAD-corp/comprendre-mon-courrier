Fines and driving licence

Letter 48, 48N or 48SI: your licence points

In short

The Ministry of the Interior writes to you when your points balance changes. The letter's name tells you the situation:

Deadlines to check

  • Letter 48: one or more points were removed. No action required.
  • Letter 48N: probationary licence with at least 3 points removed; it invites you to take a road safety course (mandatory in some cases, check the letter).
  • Letter 48SI: you have no points left and your licence is cancelled. You must return it to the prefecture within 10 clear days (jours francs).

What to do

  • Check your balance on the official « Mes points permis » service.
  • If you receive a 48N, read carefully whether the course is mandatory and by when.
  • If you receive a 48SI, stop driving and get advice quickly on possible appeals.
  • To recover points: a road safety awareness course is possible under conditions.

Watch out: Driving with a cancelled licence is a criminal offence.
