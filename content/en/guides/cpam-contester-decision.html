Health insurance (CPAM)

You disagree with a CPAM (health insurance) decision

In short

The CPAM can refuse a reimbursement, coverage or sick-pay, or ask you to repay money. The decision must say how to challenge it. The route depends on the type of decision.

Deadlines to check

  • Administrative decision (rights, reimbursements, money claimed…): write to the amicable appeals board (CRA) within 2 months, by ordinary letter. No answer within 2 months means it is rejected.
  • Medical decision (opinion of the medical adviser): write to the medical amicable appeals board (CMRA) within 2 months, by registered letter. No answer within 4 months means it is rejected.
  • Next step: the social division of the judicial court (pôle social du tribunal judiciaire) within 2 months; a €50 contribution may be required unless you get legal aid.

What to do

  • Identify the type of decision and the appeal route written on the letter.
  • Write to the board before the deadline, with the decision and your supporting documents (and, for a medical decision, the relevant medical documents).
  • After an unsuccessful complaint, you can contact the health insurance mediator: doing so suspends the appeal deadline.
  • Keep copies of everything and proof of sending.

Watch out: Never post medical documents on a forum or unofficial website. Send them only to the board or the medical service.
