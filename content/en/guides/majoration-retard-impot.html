Taxes

The tax office reminds you of a late payment

In short

If a tax is not paid on time, the tax office sends a reminder and adds a 10% surcharge. Without payment, enforcement can follow (seizure from a bank account or salary).

Deadlines to check

  • The 10% surcharge applies if the tax is not paid within 45 days after collection started.
  • A request for a payment plan gets an answer within 2 months; no answer means it is refused.

What to do

  • Pay as soon as possible to limit costs, or ask for a payment plan from your personal account on impots.gouv.fr.
  • If you paid on time, send proof of payment through secure messaging.
  • If you are in difficulty, you can ask for the surcharge to be waived, explaining your situation.
  • If the disagreement continues: the tax conciliator (conciliateur fiscal) of your département, then the mediator of the economic and financial ministries.

Watch out: Do not leave a reminder unanswered: contacting the tax office early often gets you a payment plan.
