URSSAF (social contributions)

URSSAF sends you a formal notice (mise en demeure)

In short

A formal notice (mise en demeure) is an official letter claiming unpaid social contributions or surcharges. It comes before enforced collection (the « contrainte »).

Deadlines to check

  • You have 1 month to pay or settle. After that, URSSAF can issue a contrainte (enforcement order).
  • To challenge the amount: write to URSSAF's amicable appeals board (CRA) within 2 months of receipt.

What to do

  • Check the periods and amounts claimed (missing turnover returns, estimated contributions…).
  • If a return is missing, file it: the estimated amount will be recalculated on your real income.
  • If you cannot pay everything, ask for a payment plan from your online account (messaging) or by phone.
  • If you disagree, write to the CRA before the deadline with your documents.

Watch out: Do not ignore a formal notice: the enforcement order that may follow has the effect of a court judgment.
