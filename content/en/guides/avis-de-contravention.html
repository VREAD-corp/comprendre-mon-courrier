Fines and driving licence

You received a traffic offence notice (avis de contravention)

In short

The notice tells you about a traffic offence (speeding, red light, parking…), often recorded by a camera. It shows the amount depending on when you pay.

Deadlines to check

  • Reduced fine (minorée): paid within 15 days, or 30 days if the notice was posted to you and you pay online or by card. No reduction for parking.
  • Standard fine (forfaitaire): paid within 45 days (60 days online or by card).
  • Challenge it (requête en exonération): within 45 days, online on the ANTAI website or by registered letter. A deposit of the amount is required if you dispute the offence.
  • Company vehicle: the company's representative must name the driver within 45 days; otherwise a fine for not naming the driver (up to €675) may be due.

What to do

  • Check the number plate, place, date and photo if available.
  • To pay: only on amendes.gouv.fr or with the methods shown on the notice.
  • If you were not the driver, or the vehicle was stolen or sold: use the ANTAI website with the notice number.
  • Paying the fine means you accept the offence (and lose points where applicable).

Watch out: Fraudulent text messages and emails imitate ANTAI. Never use a link received by SMS: type amendes.gouv.fr yourself.
