CAF (family benefits)

The CAF announces a check of your situation

In short

The CAF regularly checks files. Checks can be automatic (data compared with other administrations such as the tax office or France Travail), a request for documents, or an on-site visit by a sworn inspector. A check does not mean you are suspected of fraud.

Deadlines to check

  • For a document request: respect the deadline written in the letter.
  • For an appointment or visit: if the date does not suit you, tell the CAF quickly using the contact details in the letter.
  • If the check leads to an overpayment, you will receive a separate notice with its own appeal deadlines.

What to do

  • Read the CAF control charter: it describes your rights and the inspector's obligations (link below).
  • Prepare recent documents: income, lease or rent receipts, household members.
  • Answer honestly; mention any change in your situation you may have forgotten to report.
  • After the check, read the decision you receive. If you disagree, follow the appeal route written on it.

Watch out: Refusing a check or not replying can lead to your benefits being suspended.
