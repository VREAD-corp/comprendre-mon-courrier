Residence permits (ANEF, prefecture)

ANEF: filing certificate or extended-processing certificate

In short

When you apply for a residence permit online (ANEF), you receive several documents one after another. They do not all have the same value:

Deadlines to check

  • Filing certificate (attestation de dépôt): proves you applied. It does not prove legal stay, has no validity period and does not allow you to work.
  • Extended-processing certificate (attestation de prolongation d'instruction): a provisional residence document, valid for 3 months maximum, renewed until the decision. Your right to work depends on the permit you applied for.
  • Favourable decision certificate: your permit has been granted; it is also a provisional residence document until you get the card.

What to do

  • Download and keep every certificate from your ANEF account.
  • Watch the expiry date of the extended-processing certificate; a new one should appear in your account before it ends.
  • If the administration asks for more documents, reply within the deadline shown in your account.
  • Show the certificate to your employer if it allows work.

Watch out: If your certificate expires without renewal or you cannot reach the prefecture, get help quickly (France services, an association, a lawyer).
