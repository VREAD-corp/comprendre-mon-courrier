Is this letter or text really from the administration?

In short

Scammers imitate public bodies (unpaid fine, tax refund, health card renewal, CAF…) to steal your bank details or log-in details.

Deadlines to check

  • A message that rushes you (« last chance », « within 24 hours ») and contains a payment link is a warning sign.
  • Real procedures are done on official websites, by typing the address yourself (caf.fr, impots.gouv.fr, ameli.fr, amendes.gouv.fr, service-public.gouv.fr).

What to do

  • Do not click the link; log in to your personal account by typing the address yourself and check whether there really is a message.
  • Never give sensitive information (codes, full bank details) by message or phone.
  • Report fraudulent text messages by forwarding them to 33700, and emails on Signal Spam.
  • If you gave your bank details: call your bank immediately.

Watch out: Info Escroqueries (Ministry of the Interior): 0 805 805 817 (free call).
