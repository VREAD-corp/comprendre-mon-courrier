Residence permits (ANEF, prefecture)

Permit refused with an order to leave France (OQTF): act now

In short

An OQTF is a prefecture decision ordering you to leave France. It often comes with a residence permit refusal. You can challenge it before the administrative court (tribunal administratif), but the deadlines are very short.

Deadlines to check

  • General case (you are not under house arrest, in detention or in a detention centre): 1 month to go to the administrative court.
  • If you are under house arrest (assignation à résidence) or in prison: 7 days.
  • If you are held in an immigration detention centre (rétention): 48 hours.
  • These deadlines cannot be extended, and asking the prefecture to reconsider does not stop them. The deadline for your case is written in the decision (section « voies et délais de recours »).

What to do

  • Immediately read the « voies et délais de recours » section of the decision and note the date you received it.
  • Contact a lawyer or an association that helps foreign nationals the same day.
  • You can ask for legal aid (aide juridictionnelle) to cover lawyer's fees; a lawyer can also be appointed for you.
  • Gather your documents: the decision, passport, proof of presence in France, family life, work.

Watch out: Do not let the deadline pass while waiting for the prefecture to answer: only an appeal to the court within the deadline protects your rights. Deadlines changed in 2026: always check the decision itself.
