CAF (family benefits)

The CAF reminds you to file your quarterly income return

In short

If you receive RSA (minimum income) or the prime d'activité (in-work benefit), you must declare your income every three months. This letter or message reminds you that the return is due.

Deadlines to check

  • The return is due on the date shown in your CAF account; without it, your payments can stop.
  • Since 1 March 2025, your salaries are pre-filled (« montant net social »). You must check them, add other income, then submit.

What to do

  • Log in to your account on caf.fr (or the CAF – Mon Compte app).
  • Check each pre-filled amount against your payslips (line « montant net social »).
  • Add any income that is not pre-filled (allowances, pensions, self-employed income…).
  • Submit and keep the confirmation.

Watch out: If a pre-filled amount is wrong, correct it before submitting: you are responsible for your return.
