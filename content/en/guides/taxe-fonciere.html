You received a property tax notice (taxe foncière)

In short

Property tax (taxe foncière) is paid by people who own a property on 1 January of the year. The notice shows the amount and the payment deadline.

Deadlines to check

  • 2026 property tax: pay by 15 October 2026, or by 20 October 2026 at midnight if you pay online.
  • If you pay monthly by direct debit, nothing to do: debits continue.
  • You can file a claim until 31 December 2027 for the 2026 tax.

What to do

  • Check the property and that you owned it on 1 January.
  • Pay online on impots.gouv.fr or with the app; cash or cheque is only possible if the tax is €300 or less.
  • If there is a mistake (property sold, wrong surface…), file a claim: secure messaging > « Réclamation sur la taxe foncière ».

Watch out: A claim does not automatically stop the payment: pay on time to avoid a surcharge, unless a payment deferral is granted.
