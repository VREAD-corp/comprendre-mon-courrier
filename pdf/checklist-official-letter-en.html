What to do when I receive an official French letter

A 10-step checklist, a table of common deadlines and a tracking sheet.

Courrier clair — a product of READ FRANCE SAS · Version 1.0, 11 October 2026 · CC BY 4.0 · https://outils.vread.fr/comprendre-mon-courrier/en/

General information, not legal advice. Courrier clair explains types of letters; it does not read your letter or give personal advice. The deadlines written on your letter take priority. Checked on 11 October 2026.

The 10 steps

  1. Write down the date you received it (keep the envelope and any registered-mail receipt).
  2. Check that the letter is genuine: official sender, no payment link sent by SMS; if in doubt, log in to the official website yourself.
  3. Identify the sender (CAF, tax office, prefecture, CPAM, URSSAF, ANTAI…) and the reference number.
  4. Find what is being asked: information, documents, a payment, a reply?
  5. Find the deadline and the « voies et délais de recours » section (how and when to appeal). Work out the last day and put it in your diary.
  6. Gather the supporting documents and keep copies.
  7. Reply through the channel indicated (online account, secure messaging, registered letter) and keep proof of sending.
  8. If you cannot pay: ask for a payment plan or a waiver before the deadline.
  9. If you disagree: use the appeal route indicated within the deadline (claim, amicable appeals board…).
  10. If you do not understand or the deadline is very short: get free help (France services, point-justice) or a professional.
Free help: France services (france-services.gouv.fr), point-justice (3039), Défenseur des droits (09 69 39 00 00), mediators of the CAF, health insurance and the economic ministries. Scam messages: forward texts to 33700; Info Escroqueries 0 805 805 817.

The most common deadlines

BodyLetterMain deadlines
CAF (family benefits)CAF: request for supporting documentsThe deadline is written in the letter: there is no single standard deadline. Note it as soon as you receive the letter.
If you do not reply in time, the CAF can suspend your benefit payments.
CAF (family benefits)CAF: overpayment notice (indu)To challenge it: write to your CAF's amicable appeals board (CRA), usually within 2 months of the notice. The exact deadline and appeal route are written on the notice.
The CAF can only claim overpayments from the last 2 years (5 years in case of fraud or false statement).
CAF (family benefits)CAF: letter announcing a checkFor a document request: respect the deadline written in the letter.
For an appointment or visit: if the date does not suit you, tell the CAF quickly using the contact details in the letter.
CAF (family benefits)CAF: quarterly income return reminder (RSA, prime d'activité)The return is due on the date shown in your CAF account; without it, your payments can stop.
Since 1 March 2025, your salaries are pre-filled (« montant net social »). You must check them, add other income, then submit.
TaxesTaxes: understanding your income tax noticeBalance of €300 or less: taken in one payment in September. More than €300: taken in 4 payments, September to December.
To challenge or correct it: a claim (réclamation) is possible until 31 December of the 2nd year after collection started (2026 notice: until 31 December 2028).
TaxesTaxes: 2026 property tax notice (taxe foncière)2026 property tax: pay by 15 October 2026, or by 20 October 2026 at midnight if you pay online.
If you pay monthly by direct debit, nothing to do: debits continue.
TaxesTaxes: proposed tax adjustment after an auditYou have 30 days from receipt to reply.
This can be extended by 30 days if you ask and your request arrives before the first deadline ends.
TaxesTaxes: reminder and 10% late-payment surchargeThe 10% surcharge applies if the tax is not paid within 45 days after collection started.
A request for a payment plan gets an answer within 2 months; no answer means it is refused.
TaxesTaxes: housing occupancy declaration and second homesThe occupancy declaration is made in « Gérer mes biens immobiliers » on impots.gouv.fr. It was due before 1 July 2026 for owners who had never filed it or whose situation had changed; otherwise only when something changes.
2026 housing tax on second homes: pay before 15 December 2026.
Residence permits (ANEF, prefecture)Residence permit: ANEF certificates (filing, extended processing)Filing certificate (attestation de dépôt): proves you applied. It does not prove legal stay, has no validity period and does not allow you to work.
Extended-processing certificate (attestation de prolongation d'instruction): a provisional residence document, valid for 3 months maximum, renewed until the decision. Your right to work depends on the permit you applied for.
Residence permits (ANEF, prefecture)Residence permit: refusal with an order to leave France (OQTF)General case (you are not under house arrest, in detention or in a detention centre): 1 month to go to the administrative court.
If you are under house arrest (assignation à résidence) or in prison: 7 days.
Residence permits (ANEF, prefecture)Residence permit: collecting your card and the tax stampSince 1 May 2026, the fee is €350 for a first card (standard rate) or €150 (reduced rate), and €250 for a renewal (standard rate) or €100 (reduced rate). A duplicate costs €350 (€150 reduced).
Go on the date or within the period given in the invitation; if you cannot, contact the prefecture beforehand.
Health insurance (CPAM)Health insurance: challenging a CPAM decisionAdministrative decision (rights, reimbursements, money claimed…): write to the amicable appeals board (CRA) within 2 months, by ordinary letter. No answer within 2 months means it is rejected.
Medical decision (opinion of the medical adviser): write to the medical amicable appeals board (CMRA) within 2 months, by registered letter. No answer within 4 months means it is rejected.
Health insurance (CPAM)Health insurance: checks during sick leaveIf you are away during a home check without a valid reason, your daily sick pay can be suspended.
After the employer's counter-visit, the doctor informs the CPAM within 48 hours.
URSSAF (social contributions)URSSAF: formal notice to pay contributionsYou have 1 month to pay or settle. After that, URSSAF can issue a contrainte (enforcement order).
To challenge the amount: write to URSSAF's amicable appeals board (CRA) within 2 months of receipt.
URSSAF (social contributions)URSSAF: enforcement order (contrainte)You have 15 days from notification or service to file an opposition.
The opposition is filed with the registry of the competent court (social division of the judicial court where you live) or by registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt; it must give reasons and include a copy of the contrainte. The contrainte itself must state this deadline and the court.
URSSAF (social contributions)URSSAF: late surcharges (micro-entrepreneur)The « right to make a mistake » (droit à l'erreur) can allow the surcharges to be waived if you put things right within 30 days (subject to amount limits).
A payment-plan request sent through your online account messaging usually gets a quick answer; by phone or letter, allow about 15 days.
Fines and driving licenceFines: traffic offence notice (ANTAI)Reduced fine (minorée): paid within 15 days, or 30 days if the notice was posted to you and you pay online or by card. No reduction for parking.
Standard fine (forfaitaire): paid within 45 days (60 days online or by card).
Fines and driving licenceFines: increased fixed fine notice (amende majorée)Claim (réclamation): within 30 days of the notice being sent (3 months if it was sent by registered letter). You can no longer file a claim once you have started paying.
Quick payment: paying within 30 days (45 days online) gives a 20% reduction.
Fines and driving licenceDriving licence points: letters 48, 48N and 48SILetter 48: one or more points were removed. No action required.
Letter 48N: probationary licence with at least 3 points removed; it invites you to take a road safety course (mandatory in some cases, check the letter).
All public bodiesFake government letters, texts and emails: how to spot themA message that rushes you (« last chance », « within 24 hours ») and contains a payment link is a warning sign.
Real procedures are done on official websites, by typing the address yourself (caf.fr, impots.gouv.fr, ameli.fr, amendes.gouv.fr, service-public.gouv.fr).
